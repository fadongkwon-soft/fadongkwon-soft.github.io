## Numbers, or a Picture
Slide tiles into the empty square until they are in order.
When numbers get dull, switch to **picture mode** — and on the last tile you see the finished image first.

- 🔢 **Classic 15 puzzle** — slide tiles into the gap to sort them
- 🖼️ **Picture mode** — assemble an image instead of numbers, and tap the thumbnail to see it large
- 🎉 **Completion reveal** — place the last tile and the finished picture shows first
- 📅 **Daily board** — a new puzzle every day with streaks
- 🏅 **Records** — your time and move count accumulate
- 💾 **Continue anytime** — your board is auto-saved

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
