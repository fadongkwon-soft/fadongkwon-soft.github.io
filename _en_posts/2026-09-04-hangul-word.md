## Info
> **Hangul Word Guess** is a daily Korean word puzzle in the Wordle style: guess the
> hidden word one jamo (consonant or vowel) at a time across six tiles. Green, yellow
> and gray tiles show how close you are; hints, win-rate and streak stats included.
> Great for Korean learners. Offline, no sign-up.
{: .prompt-info }

## Wordle, in Hangul
This one is for everyone who played the English Wordle and wondered why there wasn't
a Korean one. **Each word is spread across six jamo tiles**, and you type consonants
and vowels one by one — which leaves far more room for deduction than a
syllable-based version would.

- 📅 **One word a day** — everyone gets the same puzzle, and a free mode lets you practice
- 🔤 **Six jamo tiles** — compound vowels and double finals are entered as separate parts (e.g. ㅘ → ㅗ + ㅏ)
- 🟩 **Green, yellow and gray tiles** — every guess shows how close you are
- 💡 **Hints** — reveal one jamo when you're stuck
- 📊 **Stats** — win rate, streaks and guess distribution are saved
- 🎓 **Good for Korean learners** — a natural way to build intuition for how jamo combine into words

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login.
