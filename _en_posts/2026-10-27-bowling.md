## About the Game
Pull back and let go to bowl. You play a real 10-frame game with one finger: slide to your spot, add a little spin and aim for the pocket.

## How to Play
- Drag the ball left or right to pick your **spot**
- Drag down anywhere and release to throw. The pull distance sets the speed
- Choose one of **five spin settings**, from a big hook to straight. The ball slides on the oil, then hooks on the dry boards
- Official 10-pin scoring: strikes, spares and the bonus balls in the 10th frame
- Pins knock each other over with physics, so splits like 7-10 and 4-6 happen

## Features
- Today's Lane: a new oil pattern every day, the same for everyone, with your daily streak and best score
- Free game on the house lane, saved so you can continue later
- Frame-by-frame score row, strike and spare calls, double and turkey streaks
- TOP 10 scores and stats: games played, average, best, total strikes
- Sound effects made on the device, with a sound on/off button
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
