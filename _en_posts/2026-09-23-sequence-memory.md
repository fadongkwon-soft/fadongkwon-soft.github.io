## Info
> **Sequence Memory** lights up a sequence of pads and asks you to repeat it, adding one more step every round. Choose 4, 6 or 9 pads, keep a streak going on the shared Daily Sequence, and track TOP 10 rounds per mode. Works offline, no sign-up. 9 languages.
{: .prompt-info }

## One More Step Every Round
The pads light up in order. Once they stop, you press the same order back.
Round one is a single pad, so everyone gets it. The trouble starts after that.
Each round adds one more pad to the end, and you have to press the whole thing again from the beginning.

- 🎚️ **Three difficulties** — 4, 6 or 9 pads
- 📅 **Daily Sequence** — one shared sequence a day for everyone, with a streak count
- 👀 **Watch, then repeat** — the round number and your progress stay on screen
- 🏅 **TOP 10 rounds per mode** — new records and rank badges included
- 📣 **Share your run** — tell a friend how many rounds you held and challenge them

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
