## Info
> **Juice Spinner** picks who pays. Choose one of nine fruits, set the table size from 2 to 12, and spin. The wheel is numbered clockwise from twelve o'clock and the winning slice turns red. Seven languages, works offline, no sign-up.
{: .prompt-info }

## It Decides the Thing Nobody Wants to Decide
Working out who pays for the round takes longer than it should.
Rock paper scissors drags on once the group is big, and drawing lots needs paper.
Here you set the number of people, spin once, and it is settled.

- 🧃 **Nine fruits** — apple, banana, grape, kiwi, mango, orange, pineapple, strawberry, watermelon
- 👥 **2 to 12 players** — the wheel splits into exactly that many slices
- 🎯 **A result you can read at a glance** — numbered clockwise from twelve o'clock, with only the winning slice in red
- 🌏 **Seven languages** — Korean, English, Chinese, Japanese, French, Italian, Spanish
- 📱 **No install needed** — on Apps in Toss it opens straight inside the Toss app

The fruit you pick changes the background and the artwork on the wheel. It is not a feature so much as a way to keep the app from feeling stale when you spin it several times in a row.

There is no sign-up or login, and it runs without a connection.
