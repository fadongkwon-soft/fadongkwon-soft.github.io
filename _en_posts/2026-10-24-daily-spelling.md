## About the Game
Daily Korean Spelling asks one Korean spelling question a day. Pick from four choices, see the answer at once and read why. Keep going with more questions whenever you like.

One honest note: the whole app, including the questions and explanations, is in Korean only. It is a quiz about Korean spelling and word spacing, so it is made for Korean learners who can read Korean and for native Korean speakers who want to sharpen their spelling.

## How to Play
- Answer **today's question** on the home screen. It is the same for everyone, and answering it builds your daily streak
- Pick one of four choices and the app grades it immediately, with an explanation
- Tap **one more question** to keep playing with random questions
- In **past questions** you can see today's record, a wrong-answer notebook (it can drop a question once you get it right) and favorites with a star
- **Stats** shows your current and best streak, questions solved, accuracy and accuracy by category
- The brag button shares today's result

## Features
- 260 original questions: word spacing, spelling, commonly confused words, standard forms and loanword spelling
- Instant grading with an explanation for every question
- Wrong-answer notebook and starred favorites
- Streak and accuracy-by-category stats

It is free to play and contains ads.
