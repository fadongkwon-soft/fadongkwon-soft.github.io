## About the Game
Spider Solitaire is played with two decks across ten columns. Stack cards down in any order of suits, but only a run in one suit moves together. Complete a full run from King to Ace and it leaves the board — clear all eight runs to win.

## How to Play
- Stack cards in descending order, whatever the suit
- Only a run in one suit can be moved together
- A full King-to-Ace run in one suit is cleared from the board automatically
- When you run out of moves, deal a new row of ten cards from the stock (the Deal button at the bottom). You can deal even when a column is empty
- Clear all eight runs to win
- If you get stuck, the game tells you and lets you undo, restart the deal or start a new one

## Difficulty
- **1 suit (Easy)**: a game with a single suit
- **2 suits (Medium)**: two suits
- **4 suits (Hard)**: four suits

## Features
- Daily Spider: the same deal for everyone, with a daily streak
- Undo your moves
- Landscape button for a bigger board, and large, easy-to-read cards
- Records for each difficulty: today, this week, this month and all time, plus your best streak
- Plays in 9 languages
- A few new games are free every day; watch an optional ad for more

It is free to play and contains ads.
