## About the Game
Place a disc so it sandwiches your opponent's discs between two of yours — every sandwiched disc flips to your color. When neither player can move, whoever has more discs wins.

- Vs Computer: Easy, Normal and Hard AI
- Pick who moves first: you, the AI or random
- 2-Player mode on the same device
- Playable squares are marked and a live disc count shows who is ahead
- No move? The turn passes automatically
- Undo your last move
- Win/loss/draw record for each difficulty
- How-to-play guide with strategy tips
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
