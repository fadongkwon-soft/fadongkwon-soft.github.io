## Straight From the Toss App, No Install
The **Apps in Toss (Toss mini app) edition** of [Hangul Monsters](/en/posts/hangul-monsters/) is out.
If you have the Toss app, it opens and runs immediately — handy if installing yet another app on
a child's phone has been more trouble than it is worth.

The Toss edition **concentrates on listening**. Every question is spoken by a native-quality
neural voice, and each correct answer catches one more monster.

- 🦉 **Consonants and vowels** — hear a sound and pick the matching letter, like ㄱ (g) or ㅏ (a) (free)
- 🐙 **Consonant names** — learn what each letter is called: 기역 (giyeok), 니은 (nieun), 디귿 (digeut) (free)
- 👾 **Ga-na-da syllables** — consonant × vowel syllables such as 가 (ga) and 나 (na), with a selectable range (free)
- 🦖🐲🧟🦑 **Four word packs** — no final consonant, with a final consonant (받침), compound vowels, double consonants (unlockable)

A round is 20 questions drawn at random, so the mix is different every time, and there are no ads.
The **sentence reading mode**, where a child reads out loud into the microphone, is on the
[Google Play edition](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fadongkwon.hangul_monsters).
