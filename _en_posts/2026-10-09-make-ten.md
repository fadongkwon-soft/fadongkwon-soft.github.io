## About the Game
Drag a rectangle over the number marbles. If the numbers inside add up to exactly 10, they pop! Cleared spots stay empty, so you can link numbers across the gaps.

- Classic: 120 seconds — how many can you clear?
- Daily Board: the same board for everyone today; your first run counts and builds your streak
- Practice: a smaller board with no timer
- Quick clears in a row add combo bonuses
- Hints point out a box you can make
- Big, easy-to-read numbers
- Records: today, this week, this month and all-time
- Pause anytime · Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
