## About the Game
FreeCell is the classic solitaire where every card is dealt face up, so skill decides the game, not luck. Use the four free cells to park cards, build each column down in alternating colors, and move all 52 cards to the foundations from Ace to King. Almost every deal can be solved.

## How to Play
- Park cards in the four free cells
- Build each column down in alternating colors
- Move several cards at once when you have enough free cells and empty columns
- Cards go to the foundations automatically when it is safe
- Move all 52 cards to the foundations from Ace to King to win
- Stuck? The game tells you when no move is left and lets you undo, restart the deal or start a new one

## Features
- Numbered deals: every game shows its deal number
- Daily FreeCell: the same deal for everyone, with a daily streak
- Undo your moves
- Landscape button for a bigger board, and large, easy-to-read cards
- Records for today, this week, this month and all time, plus your best streak
- Plays in 9 languages
- A few new games are free every day; watch an optional ad for more
