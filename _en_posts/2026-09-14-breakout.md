## No Finish Line, Just a Faster Ball
Clear a board and the next one opens, and **the ball gains a little speed every level.**
At some point your hands stop keeping up. That is the game.

- 🧱 **Levels that keep coming** — layouts change and the ball speeds up as you clear
- 🎁 **Power-ups** — extra balls · wider paddle · slow motion
- 💗 **One free revive per run** — a single miss does not end your record
- 🏅 **Best records** — the level you reached and your score are kept
- 📖 **How to play** — check what each power-up does without leaving the game
- 📤 **Share** — turn a result into an image and send it to a friend

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
