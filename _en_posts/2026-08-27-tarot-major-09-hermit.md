Compressed into a single sentence, Major Arcana 9, **the Hermit**, is the card of leaving the crowd behind and lighting your own lantern to see the path. Where the Chariot races toward its goal and Strength soothes a wild instinct, the Hermit stands in the place where you stop for a while and ask whether that whole journey has been heading the right way.

## Upright vs reversed at a glance

| Field | Upright | Reversed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core keywords | reflection, prudence, inner search, counsel | isolation, loneliness, excessive caution |
| What the lantern is for | lighting your own path forward | hidden away, shown to nobody |
| Distance from others | stepping back briefly to regroup | far enough that no one can reach you |
| What is needed | finding the answer in silence | reaching a hand back outward |

## Symbols and the number

Inside the lantern the Hermit carries, a six-pointed star is shining. That star, matter and spirit interlocked, inner and outer joined, stands for a light of wisdom he made himself rather than one somebody else lit for him. The staff in his other hand shows that experience gathered over a long journey is what holds him up now. The gray robe stripped of ornament is the posture of someone concentrating on essentials, and the snow-covered summit means a height reached alone — a solitary place, but the one with the longest view. The number 9, last of the single digits, is the stage of putting yourself in order one final time just before a cycle completes. It is the last stretch of solitary review before the turn begins with the next card, the Wheel of Fortune (10).

## Upright — a light you make yourself

Upright, the Hermit means a period of pulling back from the noise outside and looking deeply into your own thinking. The emphasis falls on finding your answer somewhere quiet instead of being swept along by other people's opinions. Insight gathered in this period sometimes comes back later as solid counsel for someone else.

## Reversed — with the lantern hidden

Reversed, the Hermit shows reflection **that has drifted into isolation**. What you needed was time to think alone, and as that time stretched on you cut the connections around you altogether. Light a lantern and show it to nobody and that light eventually wears you out too. If you draw a reversal, it does not mean stop reflecting — read it as a signal that it is time to carry the lantern back outside.

## Readings by situation

- **Career** — Upright is a period of digging deep into your own expertise and quietly building substance. Reversed can mean cutting off communication with colleagues and getting stuck in your own methods, which makes collaboration difficult.
- **Love** — Upright has time by yourself clarifying what you actually want from love. Reversed can mean keeping so much distance that the other person feels lonely, so open up a little at a time.
- **Health** — Upright is a period for reducing stimulation, listening to your body's signals and recharging. Reversed can mean your activity drops so far that your body stiffens up, so it is worth keeping at least a light walk in the routine.
- **Study & Exams** — Upright means shutting out distractions, immersing yourself deeply and seeing through to core principles. Reversed can mean sinking into peripheral details and losing sight of the full syllabus, so a balanced view is needed.
- **Relationships** — Upright is a period of deep conversation and sincere advice given to a small circle. Reversed can mean bringing isolation on yourself by cutting off contact, so the small step of reaching out first helps.

## The one piece of advice this card offers

What the Hermit offers comes down to one line. **Before you look for the answer outside, put the question to yourself first.** Before chasing someone else's advice or the method everyone is using, you need quiet time to ask what you genuinely want out of the current situation. Once you have that answer, the turn to walk back out with the lantern in hand arrives on its own.
