## About the Game
Tap a column and your disc drops to the lowest empty space. Be the first to line up four of your discs — across, down or diagonally — on the 7×6 board.

- Vs Computer: Easy, Normal and Hard AI
- Pick who moves first: you, the AI or random
- 2-Player mode on the same device
- Tap anywhere in a column to drop a disc; the winning four lights up
- Undo your last move
- Win/loss/draw record for each difficulty
- How-to-play guide with strategy tips
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
