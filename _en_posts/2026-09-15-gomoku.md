## Gomoku for Two People and One Phone
Alone, the computer plays you. **Together, you put one phone between you.**
It is the game people used to play in the back of a notebook.

- 🤖 **Three computer levels** — Easy · Normal · Hard
- 👥 **Two-player** — take turns on a single phone
- ↩️ **Undo** — take back a stone you placed by mistake
- 🏅 **Record** — wins, draws and losses accumulate
- 🎯 **Last move marker** — never lose track of where your opponent played
- 📤 **Share** — turn a result into an image and send it to a friend

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
