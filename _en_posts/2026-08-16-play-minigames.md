## Starting a mini-game series
This is a series of very small games built with plain web technology — Vite and TypeScript,
no Flutter. The ground rules are the same for every title: around 2MB installed, works entirely
offline, no login and no personal data collected. Each game ships to both Google Play and
Apps in Toss (as a Toss mini app), one after the other.

## ⚡ Reaction Challenge
Tap the moment the screen turns green. Your reaction time is averaged over five rounds and
graded — from cheetah down to sloth. Which one are you?

- Five-round average, so a single lucky tap does not decide it
- TOP 10 records, each stamped with the date and time you set it
- 9 languages

## 🃏 Memory Cards
Classic pair matching — flip the cards and find all 8 pairs. The timer starts the instant you
flip the first card, so go for the fastest time and the fewest tries.

- 4x4 board, one to two minutes per round
- TOP 10 records for both time and number of tries
- 9 languages
