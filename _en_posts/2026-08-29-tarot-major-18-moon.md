A crayfish crawls out of the pool beside the road, and a dog and a wolf stand side by side, both baying up at the moon. Nothing in the picture is sharp; every shape wavers and blurs under the moonlight. Major Arcana **18, The Moon**, draws exactly this — a night road you cannot be sure of.

## Reading the symbols

- **The two towers** — the boundary of the familiar world. The only way to the next stage runs along the path between them.
- **The moon spilling light** — unlike the sun, the moon does not shine on its own; it sends back reflected light only. That hints that what you are looking at right now may not be the whole truth, but information that has been bounced around and bent along the way.
- **The crayfish in the pool** — a feeling or a fear that has not yet risen all the way to the surface of consciousness.
- **The dog and the wolf** — a tamed instinct and an untamed one, baying side by side at the same moon. It stands for a state where reason and instinct are mixed together.
- **The winding path** — a road with a destination that cannot be walked in a straight line. It means time has to pass before a clear answer arrives.

## Upright meaning

Upright, the Moon means **a state where not everything has surfaced yet**. Something is unsettled, information is short, feelings are tangled, and judgment blurs easily in the mix. This is not a card warning you to be anxious, though. It sits closer to a card that recommends **listening to intuition rather than logic**. Rather than forcing into view what cannot be seen yet, the way through this stretch is to keep your senses wide open until the fog lifts.

## Reversed meaning

Reversed, the Moon points to the phase where that fog clears. **Confusion lifts, misunderstandings come undone.** What was uncertain turns definite, and the substance behind the things you doubted shows itself. The fog clearing does not mean everything gets revealed the way you hoped, so what it asks of you is to take the truth that surfaces exactly as it is.

## Readings by situation

### 💕 Love

- **Upright** — a stretch where the other person's real feelings are hard to read and your own heart wobbles. Better to give it time and watch than to grow a suspicion prematurely.
- **Reversed** — the misunderstanding or the vague unease that has been weighing on you comes undone. Rebuild trust with an honest conversation.

### 🤝 Relationships

- **Upright** — hard to tell what the people around you truly mean, so misunderstandings pile up easily. What matters is not letting unconfirmed rumors pull you around.
- **Reversed** — misunderstanding and distrust in your circle lift and the truth comes out. A good time to talk through the resentment that has collected.

### 💼 Career

- **Upright** — office rumors or an opaque personnel policy can leave you uneasy. Rather than being swayed by hearsay, keep quietly building your skills and watch.
- **Reversed** — misunderstandings and uncertain situations at work get sorted out and the truth comes out. Respond honestly, in line with what has become clear.

### ✨ Today's fortune

- **Upright** — a day that feels like walking through fog, with your mind unsettled. Better to hold important decisions until your feelings have settled.
- **Reversed** — a day that turns noticeably lighter as a stifling misunderstanding or worry lifts. Move freely now that the situation is clear.

## If you drew this card today

Today is a good day to watch things drift along blurry for a while instead of straining to sort everything out cleanly. Do not force a conclusion on anything you are not certain about. Reversed, though, the answer to something you have been wondering about may surface on its own unexpectedly, so it is worth paying a little more attention than usual.
