## Info
> **Tarot Fortune** is a one-card tarot reading app: pick a theme, draw a card and
> read upright/reversed interpretations written for that theme. 78 cards × 12 themes,
> advice cards, 9 languages. Free with ads.
{: .prompt-info }

## Pick a theme, draw one card
Choose one of **12 themes** — love, career, money, study and more — then draw a card,
and you get an interpretation written for that theme. Upright and reversed are handled
separately, which means **78 cards × 12 themes × upright/reversed = 1,872 readings**,
all of which I wrote and reviewed myself.

- 🃏 **One-card reading** — pick your own card from a fanned-out spread
- 🧭 **12 themes** — today's fortune, love, marriage, relationships, career, business, money, investment, real estate, study, family, health
- ➕ **Advice cards** — if the result leaves you unsatisfied, watch an ad to add up to 2 advice cards (3 in total, the way clarification cards work in a real reading)
- 🌏 **9 languages** — Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese and more, with every reading translated
- 🔁 Recently drawn cards are held back for a while, so each draw brings something new

The readings come from the same world as the [Tarot Card Meanings — All 78 Cards](/en/tarot/)
on this blog, so when a card makes you curious you can go read the deeper version there.
