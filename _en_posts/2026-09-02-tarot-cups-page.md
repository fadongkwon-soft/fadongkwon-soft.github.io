## Reading it by suit and number

In the traditional image the Page of Cups stands at the shore, gazing quietly into the cup in his hand, and a fish is poking its head out of it. Surprise and delight are mixed in his expression. That fish stands for a feeling or a flash of inspiration that surfaced from deep in the unconscious without warning. Among the court cards the Page comes first of the four ranks — Page, Knight, Queen, King — and stands for learning, news and a beginner's mind. Add Cups, the suit of water, and it reads as a signal of feeling that is pure, if not yet refined. The still sea shows that the feeling is a tender one, not yet caught up in rough water.

Upright, the Page of Cups means a period when kind news or the careful signal of a crush reaches you. It is too early to be certain, and something is clearly knocking at your heart. Reversed points to sharper mood swings, or to feelings expressed so clumsily that they invite misunderstanding. In that case the work is not to press the feeling down but to practice delivering it in a calmer way.

## Readings by situation

**Love.** Upright has careful, fresh signals of attraction passing warmly back and forth. It is early to call it a relationship, but there is a good chance real feeling sits behind each small gesture. Reversed shows clumsy expression inviting misunderstanding, or a small hurt sulked over until things turn awkward.

**Study & Exams.** Upright is a period of showing unusual creativity in fields that call for feeling, like art or the humanities. It is also a time when pure curiosity works as the strongest engine for learning. Reversed lets the amount you get through swing with the mood of the day, so setting a minimum rule you can keep regardless of how you feel helps.

**Relationships.** Upright is a period of approaching someone new carefully, with a pure and warm attitude. One sincere look leaves a bigger impression than any amount of clever talk. Reversed points to a small remark wounding you and closing the door, so working things out calmly in conversation beats leading with feeling.

**Today's fortune.** Upright fills the day with good news or a message that moves you. Reversed is a day when small things make you touchy and the swings in mood show on the outside, so taking a little time alone to settle yourself is the better move.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. If the Page of Cups comes up, does it mean the person I like is about to confess?**
A. It cannot be stated that firmly. This card shows a signal of feeling that has not ripened, not a settled outcome. What words or actions it actually turns into depends on what follows and on the choices both people make.

**Q. How is the Page of Cups different from the Ace of Cups?**
A. If the Ace of Cups is the moment feeling itself first wells up, the Page is the stage where that feeling reaches someone else as a signal or a piece of news. If the Ace is the seed, the Page is closer to the moment that seed is first passed on.
