## Info
> **Tower Stack** is a timing game: every tap drops a floor, and missing shaves the block down. Power-ups you can switch off, separate records for height and score, and a stop button that banks your run. Works offline, no sign-up. 9 languages.
{: .prompt-info }

## Every Miss Narrows the Next Floor
Tap as the block slides past and it lands there. **Whatever hangs over gets shaved off,**
and the narrower block becomes the next floor's difficulty. An early slip follows you to the top.

- 👆 **One tap, one floor** — where you stop it is the whole skill
- 🎁 **Power-ups on or off** — decide whether the drifting capsules are in play
- 🏅 **Two record boards** — compete on height and on score separately
- 🛑 **Stop anytime** — end the run yourself and bank it as a record
- 📖 **Collection** — see what each power-up does from inside the game
- 💾 **Continue anytime** — your run is auto-saved

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
