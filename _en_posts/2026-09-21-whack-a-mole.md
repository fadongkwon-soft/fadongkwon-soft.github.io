## Sixty Seconds. How Many Can You Catch?
Moles pop out of the holes. Tap one and your score goes up.
That is the whole rule, and yet the moment you start rushing your hand moves first. Bombs included.

- 🐹 **Mole +1** — the basic score
- ✨ **Gold mole +3** — missing one while it sparkles stings
- 💣 **Bomb −2** — the faster you go, the less you see it
- 🎚️ **Three difficulties** — Easy, Normal, Hard
- 🔥 **Combos** — consecutive hits keep the counter climbing
- 🏅 **Six rank badges** — from Sprout Farmer to Mole King
- 🧾 **Result card** — score, best combo, moles caught, golds and bombs on one screen

Scores are kept in a TOP 10 per difficulty, and you can share yours to challenge a friend.

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
