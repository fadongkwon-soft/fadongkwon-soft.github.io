## Reading the symbols

- **The ornate lidded cup** — where the queens of the other three suits hold their suit's symbol plainly in hand, the Queen of Cups' vessel has a lid on it. It stands for the ability to feel something and still not spill it carelessly, to hold what you feel with care.
- **The throne set at the shoreline** — placed on the border between the conscious and the unconscious, one foot on the land of reason and one in the sea of feeling.
- **Waves that almost, but never quite, reach the throne** — a fine sense of balance, never fully overrun by emotion yet always feeling the swell close by.
- **The armrest carved with angels** — a pure protective instinct and a kindly heart.

## Upright meaning

Upright, the Queen of Cups is the most inwardly ripened figure among the court cards. The seat of queen already stands for maturity built up on the inside rather than achievement on display, and adding the suit of emotion to it comes out as a gift for empathy and a settled emotional life. If the card comes up upright, it means a period when you can read the people around you deeply and handle your own feelings comfortably too.

## Reversed meaning

Reversed, the Queen of Cups points to a state where that gift for empathy is what wears you out. You have gone so far into someone else's feelings that the line blurs — where their emotion ends and yours begins is no longer clear. Here you need to fold up the impulse to care for other people for a while and give your own feelings that attention first.

## Readings by situation

### 💕 Love

- **Upright** — a current where deep empathy and consideration put the other person at ease. One kind word of comfort makes the relationship noticeably warmer.
- **Reversed** — a period when going too far into the other person's feelings leaves you hurt on your own. Keep the emotional boundary and look after your own heart along the way.

### 👪 Family

- **Upright** — a period of holding a family member's pain with kindness, and lifting them with listening and empathy.
- **Reversed** — family troubles can wear you out if you take them on too personally, so it is better to keep a healthy psychological distance.

### 💼 Career

- **Upright** — a period of playing mentor, feeling a colleague's difficulty with them and adding psychological steadiness to the team.
- **Reversed** — getting pulled too far into colleagues' emotions can cloud your actual judgment at work, so take care.

### 🤝 Relationships

- **Upright** — a period of listening warmly to whatever is troubling the people around you, carried by a real gift for empathy.
- **Reversed** — going too deep into someone else's sadness pulls your own mood down with it, so hold a reasonable psychological distance.

## If you drew this card today

Today is a good day to listen a little more closely to the people around you. It is also the kind of day where you spend all of yourself on other people's care and never get around to your own, so when you close the day out, check in on how you are actually feeling. Reversed, it is better to set other people's troubles aside just for today and take some time alone.
