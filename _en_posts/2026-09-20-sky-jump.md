## Info

{: .prompt-info }

## There Is Nothing to Do but Go Up
Land on a platform and you bounce on your own. The only input is **left and right.**
The floor keeps disappearing below you, so the moment you stop climbing is the end.

- 🦘 **Automatic jumping** — land on a platform and you bounce without tapping
- 🎁 **Power-ups** — jetpack · spring · shield · magnet
- 📱 **Tilt controls** — steer by tilting the phone instead of touching the screen
- 🎛️ **Power-ups on or off** — keep only the coins and climb on your own
- 📖 **Collection** — every power-up you meet is added to the book
- 💗 **One free revive per run** — a single fall does not end your record

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
