## About the Game
A traditional Korean board game. Throw four yut sticks and move your pieces around the board — the first player to bring all four pieces home wins.

- Throw results: Do 1, Gae 2, Geol 3, Yut 4 and Mo 5 steps — Yut and Mo earn another throw
- Back-do: a throw that sends a piece one step back, which you can switch on or off on the home screen
- Shortcuts: stop exactly on a corner or the center to cut across the diagonal
- Stack your pieces to move them together, or capture an opponent’s piece to send it back to the start and throw again
- Use each result on any piece, in any order
- Three computer levels: Easy, Normal and Hard
- Two players can take turns on one device
- Choose who goes first: you, the computer or random
- Win-loss record for each level
- Built-in how-to-play guide
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
