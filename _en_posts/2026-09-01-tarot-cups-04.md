There are moments when you have grown so used to what you already hold that you fail to notice the chance that has just arrived. The **Four of Cups** draws exactly that indifference.

## Upright vs reversed at a glance

| Field | Upright | Reversed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emotional state | boredom, listlessness, a stability that does not satisfy | the will to climb out of the listlessness |
| Attitude | letting an offer or a kindness pass unnoticed | taking the offer or the chance back up |
| Key advice | do not treat what is in front of you as given | reach a hand out with an open mind |
| In relationships | easy to grow careless with the person beside you | a chance to repair the relationship arrives |

## Symbols and the number

In the traditional image, a figure sits under a tree with folded arms, looking at three cups on the ground in front of him with no interest at all. Above them a hand reaches out of a cloud offering a fourth cup, and he does not so much as glance at it. Across the Minor Arcana the number 4 stands for stability and stagnation. Having already taken hold of three cups — the celebration and plenty of the Three of Cups — he lacks nothing, and the Four of Cups shows that very sense of security hardening into listlessness. Because Cups belong to the water element, what is distinctive here is that the stagnation comes from no outside circumstance at all but purely from **feeling and frame of mind**. The folded arms stand for a defensive, closed attitude, and the back leaning against the tree reveals a wish to stay in the familiar spot rather than take in anything new.

## Upright meaning

Upright, the Four of Cups points to letting a chance or a kindness drift past as though it were nothing much. Nothing is wrong, nothing is exciting either, and that is what makes the stagnation more dangerous. Growing so used to what is already beside you that you forget its worth is this card's central trap.

## Reversed meaning

Reversed is the slow climb out of that listlessness. Like unfolding the closed arms and accepting the fourth cup the hand in the cloud is holding out, you start to welcome a new proposal or an outstretched hand again. It can be read as a sign that you have noticed the stagnation yourself and begun to move.

## Readings by situation

- **Love** — Upright is a period when familiarity breeds boredom, or the other person's kindness slips past you unnoticed. Reversed opens the door again, ready to accept a new connection or an approach being made.
- **Family** — Upright makes it easy to take the family's familiar care for granted and let it pass. Reversed has you gladly taking up their interest and their suggestions.
- **Marriage** — Upright is a period when repeating routine turns into boredom and you can miss your partner's consideration. Reversed finds you ready to accept a fresh turn in the marriage.
- **Career** — Upright is a time when repetitive work feels stale and it is easy to turn away from a new proposal. Reversed lifts you out of the slump, with your drive and motivation coming back.
- **Today's fortune** — Upright is a day with no appetite for anything, and it drags. Reversed is a good day to shake off the listlessness and try one small change.

## The one piece of advice the Four of Cups offers

Look back and ask whether you are treating what is beside you right now as simply given. The figure in the Four of Cups is not unhappy. He has only gone numb to what he already holds, to the point of missing a good chance that has just arrived. Unfold your arms, lift your head, and check once what the fourth cup being held out in front of you actually is. That is the only advice this card offers.
