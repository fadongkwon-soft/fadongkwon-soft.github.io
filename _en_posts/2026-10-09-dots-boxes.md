## About the Game
Take turns drawing a line between two neighboring dots — draw a box's fourth side to claim it and move again. When every line is drawn, whoever claimed more boxes wins.

- Vs Computer: Easy, Normal and Hard AI
- Pick who moves first: you, the AI or random
- 2-Player mode on the same device
- Three board sizes: 3×3, 4×4 and 5×5 boxes
- Lines that can close a box are highlighted on your turn
- Undo your last move
- Win/loss/draw record for each board size and difficulty
- How-to-play guide with strategy tips
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
