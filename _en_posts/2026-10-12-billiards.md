## About the Game
Pocket and carom billiards you play with one finger. Sink the 8-ball or the 9-ball, build points in Four-Ball and Three-Ball, or take on the three-cushion shot — five games in one app.

I wrote the physics myself so the balls roll, bounce off the cushions and carry spin like real ones. Even the numbered balls turn in the direction they roll.

## How to Play
- Tap or drag on the table to aim. Pick a long or short aim line and fine-tune with the left and right arrows
- Pull the power bar and release to shoot
- Choose the contact point on the cue ball for follow, draw and side spin
- 8-Ball uses call shots (ball and pocket), 9-Ball has the push-out and the three-foul rule — standard WPA rules

## Features
- Five games: 8-Ball, 9-Ball, Four-Ball, Three-Ball and 3-Cushion
- Vs Computer on Easy, Normal or Hard, 2 players on one device, 2v2 team matches and a practice mode
- Win/loss record for every game and difficulty
- A how-to-play guide for every game
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads. A full-screen ad appears between matches from time to time.
