## Info
> **Star Sign Lotto** finds your sign from your birth date and folds that sign's lucky numbers into each pick. Two of the six come from the sign, the rest from your birth date, and the ones that came from the sign are marked on the balls.
{: .prompt-info }

> The content is Korean only — questions, explanations and copy are all written in Korean, and the lottery draws it checks against are the Korean ones. It is listed in Korea.
{: .prompt-warning }

## Two From the Sign, Four From the Date
Enter a birth date and the app works out which of the twelve signs it falls under.
Each sign carries three lucky numbers, and every game takes two of them. The other four come from the date itself.

People who share a sign share those lucky numbers; different birthdays still give different combinations.
The balls that came from the sign are marked, and a one-line note explains the pick.

- ✨ **Birth date only** — pick a year, month and day, and that is the setup
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Several profiles** — keep family and friends' signs alongside yours
- 🎰 **Two lotteries** — Korean Lotto 6/45 and Pension Lottery 720+
- 🔖 **Saved numbers** — checked automatically against every past draw
- 📊 **Your luck, replayed** — re-runs your picks across every past round and counts the results

> Numbers are for fun. Nothing is guaranteed, and the app is not affiliated with any official lottery operator.
{: .prompt-warning }
