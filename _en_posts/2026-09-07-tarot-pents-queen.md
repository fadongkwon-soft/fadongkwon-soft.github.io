"Lately I only feel settled if the housework and the work at the office both go through me." "I only relax once my child's health and the household budget have passed through my own hands." If that is the frame of mind you are living in day to day, there is a good chance the Queen of Pentacles turns up in your reading. Seated in the middle of a rich garden, looking at the coin on her lap the way you would look at a child, this queen wears the face of someone who tends practical things attentively.

> The Queen of Pentacles is a figure who has learned the earth element in her body. In terms of court-card maturity she is past the Page's learning and the Knight's execution — the stage where that capability has soaked far enough inward to leave room for looking after other people.

## Reading it by suit and number

Court cards carry no number, but they do carry rank. If the Page is learning and the Knight is execution, the Queen is the stage where that execution has become second nature and there is now room to look after other people. Where the King is outward authority pointed at an organization or a society, the Queen is closer to inward maturity pointed at a home and the relationships nearest to it. Meeting the element of Pentacles (earth), this maturity takes a particularly caretaking form. The garden around her with rabbits playing in it, the gaze resting on the coin in her lap — all of it shows that the way she handles the material is closer to tending than to owning.

## Upright — the ease of a caretaking hand

Upright, the Queen of Pentacles is someone who holds a practical sense and a warm care at the same time. Whether it is a household budget or the running of a team, the balance at the heart of this card is missing nothing that needs attention while never losing her ease. Looking closely not in order to control but because she cares — if this card comes up upright, it means that touch of yours is giving the people around you a sense of steadiness right now.

## Reversed — the hand worn out from caring

Reversed, the Queen of Pentacles is closer to a state where the caring has gone so far that none is left for herself. A perfectionism that only settles once everything has passed through her own hands ends up breaking the balance between work and life; or, the other way around, an urge to control even the smallest thing wears out everyone nearby. If it comes up reversed, it is time to put down the habit of carrying it all alone, and to add yourself to the list of people being looked after.

## Readings by situation

Whether it is the strength to hold a home together or the instinct to keep an eye on an organization, the Queen of Pentacles shows most clearly wherever someone is being looked after in a real, practical way. In the area of **family**, upright means tending your family's health and household with an attentive hand, while reversed can turn that attentiveness into nagging or control that wears the family out, so some practice at letting go is needed. In **career**, upright leads a team steadily with an excellent practical sense and a capacity to include people, while reversed makes it easy to take everything on alone and lose your balance, so sharing responsibility with colleagues is the better move. In **health**, upright looks after your body with seasonal food and regular habits, while reversed can make you so exacting about diet and management that the pleasure goes out of it, so the ease has to be recovered. In **property and home**, upright is a season of drawing real satisfaction from making a snug, generous space, while reversed can leave everyone short of air as you try to manage even your family's own space, so each person's territory has to be respected.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. Does the Queen of Pentacles only mean a "mother" or a "homemaker"?**
No. Regardless of gender or role, it points to the attitude itself — attending to practical things carefully and managing them well. A team lead who keeps the team's affairs in order, or a practitioner who never misses a detail of a project, can be read with this card too.

**Q. How is the Queen of Pentacles different from the Nine of Pentacles?**
Where the woman in the Nine of Pentacles is quietly enjoying a harvest of her own, the Queen of Pentacles is the stage of sharing that ease past herself and out to the people around her. If the 9 is independence, the Queen is care built on top of independence.
