"Everything I try lately keeps grinding — when does this run of bad luck break?" "Something that was going well has suddenly changed direction. Should I get on now?" If you are holding a question like that in a reading and you meet card 10, **the Wheel of Fortune**, the card tells you one thing before it answers you. The wheel does not know how to stop. Whether you are on the way up or the way down right now, the next notch is already turning.

> The Wheel of Fortune is Major Arcana 10, the first double-digit card after the single-digit journey from 1 to 9 comes to an end. Upright it means a turning point, good fortune, a change in the current; reversed it means stagnation, unexpected variables, timing that fails to line up.

## Reading the wheel through its number and symbols

In the traditional Rider-Waite deck, the wheel carries letters along its rim (an arrangement read as TARO or as ROTA), alchemical signs and Hebrew letters, and in the four corners sit an angel reading a book, an eagle, a lion and a bull. Those four stand for the fixed signs of astrology — Aquarius, Scorpio, Leo and Taurus — meaning the underlying order that does not change no matter how fast the wheel spins. On the left side of the wheel a serpent slides downward while on the right a jackal-headed Anubis rises, and that composition, ascent and descent present in one picture at the same time, is exactly what luck is. At the top a sphinx sits holding a sword, standing for the wisdom that keeps its center even on a wheel in motion.

By the numbers, 10 is the point where the first journey running from 1 to 9 wraps up and moves into a new digit. If 1 (the Magician) was a journey begun by personal will, 10 (the Wheel) is the moment that will meets a larger current it cannot control. And since 1+0 returns to 1, the Wheel also carries the sense of a start on a completely different footing.

### Upright — when the current turns your way

Upright, the Wheel points to a period when outside circumstances mesh in your favor. Timing falls into place without you having to force anything, and something that had been blocked opens up through an unexpected opening. Still, the good fortune this card describes is not something that happens to drop out of the sky — it is closer to a result produced by the choices you have been stacking up until now. What matters is whether you are ready to take the opportunity when the wheel comes back around to your side.

### Reversed — when the gears slip

Reversed means less that the current itself is bad and more that the timing is off. Your planned schedule and the actual situation keep diverging, and the results of your effort show up at a different moment than you expected. Rather than trying to force the wheel around, the realistic move is to keep flexible alternatives ready until the next notch comes.

## Readings by situation

**Career** — Upright points to a moment when the current of your career changes, like a transfer or a promotion. Even with an abrupt change, preparing to ride it works better than resisting. Reversed is a state where reassignments or work timing keep failing to line up, so it takes patience to watch and wait until things settle instead of rushing.

**Money** — Upright is a period when a blocked cash flow opens up through an unexpected opening. A bonus or income you did not count on may arrive, but using it as a foothold beats spending it without a plan in the excitement. Reversed brings unexpected expenses that shake the household budget, so I would recommend keeping a generous cash buffer.

**Love** — Upright is a time when the relationship reaches a natural turning point and moves along smoothly. Letting things take their course rather than pushing actually makes the relationship more comfortable. Reversed is a period when plans and feelings keep missing each other, so it helps to wait for the next opportunity instead of trying to force a match.

**Investing** — Upright points to a phase where a large market cycle is turning. What is needed is the sense to read the direction the current is shifting and adjust your stance, rather than swinging with short-term results. Reversed is a time when entry timing easily falls out of step with the market, so the principle of waiting for the next current matters more than trying to make it back.

**Today's fortune** — Upright means a day when welcome news arrives out of nowhere. Take even small coincidences lightly and gladly. Reversed means a day when the schedule keeps slipping, so leaving slack in your plan beats packing it tight.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. Is the Wheel of Fortune always a good card?**
A. No. This card is less about good or bad than about announcing the fact that something is changing. Even upright, what kind of change is coming needs the surrounding cards and the context of the question to read accurately.

**Q. If it comes up reversed, does my plan fall apart completely?**
A. It is closer to a delay or a change of order than a collapse. It does not mean sudden ruin the way the Tower does — the accurate reading is that the moment the gears engage turns out different from what you expected.
