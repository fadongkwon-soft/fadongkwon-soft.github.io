## About the Game
FDK Board Duel collects 16 board games: Korean games such as Janggi, mini Go, Gomoku, Yut Nori and Gonu, plus Reversi, Checkers, Four in a Row, Mancala, Backgammon, Morris, Hex, Amazons, Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe, Sea Battle and Dots and Boxes. Pick any of them from the home screen without installing a separate app for each.

Play against the computer and pick a difficulty for each game. Win 3 daily matches to raise your rank.

## How to Play
- Tap the board game you want on the home screen
- Play against the computer, with a difficulty to pick for each game
- Win 3 daily matches to raise your rank
- See one combined rank built from your wins and losses in every game on the home screen

## Features
- 16 board games in one app
- 5 Korean games and 11 world board games
- Play the computer, with a difficulty for each game
- Win 3 daily matches to raise your rank
- One combined rank from every game
