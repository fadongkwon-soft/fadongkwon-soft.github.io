## Info

## Flip, Remember, Match
Turn two cards over. Same picture and they stay; different and they flip back. That is the whole rule.
What this version adds is that **it times you and counts your flips.** Turning cards at random will still finish the board, but it will not leave a record.

- 🎚️ **Three sizes** — Easy 6 pairs (4×3), Normal 8 pairs (4×4), Hard 10 pairs (4×5)
- ⏱️ **Time and flips** — both numbers stay on screen while you play
- 🏅 **TOP 10 records** — kept per size, with your rank shown on a new best
- 🍓 **Fruit faces** — bright and distinct, which makes positions easier to hold
- 📣 **Share your clear** — tell a friend the time they have to beat

Because the whole game runs on concentration, there are no ads inside a round.

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login.
