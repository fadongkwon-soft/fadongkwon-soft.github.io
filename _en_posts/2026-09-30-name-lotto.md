## Split a Name Into Letters and an Element Shows Up
Break a Korean name into its consonants and vowels and you can read it as the five elements —
wood, fire, earth, metal and water. Type a name and the balance appears as bars straight away.

The strongest element picks the number band: wood 1–9, fire 10–18, earth 19–27, metal 28–36, water 37–45.
Each game takes two numbers from that band and four from the name itself. Band numbers are marked on the balls.

- ✍️ **One name** — type it and the element bars fill in immediately
- 🎨 **Numbers with a reason** — two from the dominant element's band
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Several names** — keep the family's and switch between them
- 🔖 **Saved numbers** — checked automatically against every past draw
- 📊 **Your luck, replayed** — re-runs your picks across every past round and counts the results

> Numbers are for fun. Nothing is guaranteed, and the app is not affiliated with any official lottery operator.
