## You Know the Rules. The Opponent Knows You
Pick rock, paper or scissors on the count. That part needs no explanation.
What changes is **Master**, which remembers the hands you have thrown and plays the next one against you.
People repeat the same hand more often than they think, and most of us switch hands right after losing.

- 🎲 **Random** — an opponent running on pure luck
- 🧠 **Master** — an opponent that reads your patterns and counters them
- 👥 **Two players** — sit across one phone, first to 3 wins
- 🏅 **TOP 10 streaks** — kept separately for each level
- 📊 **Record and win rate** — wins, draws and losses all tracked
- 📣 **Share your streak** — send a friend a challenge

In two-player matches your pick stays hidden on screen.
If the person across from you could see it there would be no game, so both hands are revealed at once after both players choose.

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
