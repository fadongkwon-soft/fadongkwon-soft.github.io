Seven cups float in the clouds, and a figure stands in front of them in silhouette, head tilted up. Each one holds something different — a glowing face, jewels, a laurel wreath, a castle, a snake, a dragon, and one shape wrapped in a veil. The **Seven of Cups** draws exactly this — the moment when too many possibilities are on offer and not one of them gets chosen.

## Reading the symbols

- **The seven cups** — each one holding something different, they stand for the crowd of options in front of you. In the Minor Arcana the number 7 carries the sense of testing and evaluation, and in the Seven of Cups that test turns into a test of discernment — telling the real thing from the merely convincing one.
- **Shapes floating on cloud** — the key detail is that the contents sit on cloud rather than on solid ground. This is the territory of imagination and possibility, nothing of it yet in hand.
- **The cup with a snake, the cup with a dragon** — a warning that danger and traps are mixed in among the options that look most alluring. The picture tells you itself that not every cup is a good choice.
- **The figure drawn as a silhouette** — with no clear expression and no clear direction, the shadow shape shows someone who has not settled on any of it yet.

## Upright meaning

Upright, the Seven of Cups describes **a state where the options are so plentiful that only imagination and deliberation keep growing**. There is a great deal you want to do and several directions your heart leans toward, and none of them gets carried out. True to the Cups suit, this confusion usually starts in emotional pull, so a vague expectation or a fantasy clouds the judgment more easily than logic ever does. It is worth remembering, too, that some of what sits in those cups hides a snake or a dragon behind an attractive surface.

## Reversed meaning

Reversed, the clouded fantasy is stripped away and **everything starts getting sorted into realistic choices**. This is the stage of working out which of the seven vague possibilities you can actually get your hands on, and which is only a plausible daydream. Watching the list shrink can feel like a loss, but that pruning is exactly what gives you the strength to act.

## Readings by situation

### 💕 Love

- **Upright** — a period of weighing several possible connections, or of building a fantasy about someone. Instead of chasing shapes in the clouds, look calmly at what the person is actually like.
- **Reversed** — unrealistic expectations clear away and you see the relationship for what it is. Sort out the unnecessary worrying and concentrate on the one choice your heart genuinely responds to.

### 🤝 Relationships

- **Upright** — easy to chase a flashy network or fall for a fantasy about people. What you need is an eye for the person with real character rather than the best surface.
- **Reversed** — a good time to skim the froth off connections with nothing in them and pick out who truly belongs beside you. Depth of friendship serves you better than breadth of network.

### 💼 Career

- **Upright** — so many things you want to do and so many options open that you drift instead of focusing. Clear out the castles in the air and narrow down to goals you can actually reach.
- **Reversed** — points toward tidying away unrealistic schemes and settling on a career goal that holds up. Start with whatever has the clearest priority and move step by step.

### 💰 Money

- **Upright** — so much you want to own and spend on that idle daydreaming comes easily. Better to cover what you genuinely need than to chase wants you cannot fund.
- **Reversed** — leans toward sorting out vague spending urges and building a realistic buying plan. Take care of the high-priority items first and spend carefully.

## If you drew this card today

Today may be a day when an unusual number of things you want to do and places you want to go come to mind. Try to hold all of them at once, though, and the real risk is that the day passes without a single one of them started. Pick exactly one item off the list in your head and turn it into a small action before the day is out. Reversed, you have already sorted the answer out in your own mind, so trust that judgment and let the remaining options go without regret.
