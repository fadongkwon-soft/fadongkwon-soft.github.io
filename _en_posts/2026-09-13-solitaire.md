## A Card Game With Nothing New to Learn
I wanted one game that needs no instructions.
So I left the rules alone and spent the time **on the taps you shouldn't have to make.**

- 🃏 **Classic Klondike** — the rules exactly as you remember them
- ✨ **Auto-collect** — send every playable card up in a single tap
- ↩️ **Undo** — step back one move when you are stuck
- 📅 **Daily deal** — a new deal each day, the same one for everyone
- 🏅 **Records** — clear time and move count accumulate
- 💾 **Continue anytime** — your game is auto-saved

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
