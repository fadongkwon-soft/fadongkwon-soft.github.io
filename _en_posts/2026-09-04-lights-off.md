## Press One, Four Flip
Turn off every light on the grid and you clear the stage. It sounds simple, but
**pressing a light also flips the ones above, below, left and right** — tap without
thinking and the board only gets brighter. Once the rule clicks, a few boards on the
commute disappear in no time.

- 💡 **Toggle rule** — the cell you tap and its four neighbors all flip
- 🗺️ **Level select** — stages get trickier as you go; cleared levels show their stars and can be replayed
- ⭐ **Three-star challenge** — solve within par moves for three stars, and watch your total grow on the home screen
- 📅 **Daily board** — a fresh puzzle every day, always with a guaranteed solution
- 🎯 **Hints** — when you're stuck, a hint marks exactly which light to press

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
