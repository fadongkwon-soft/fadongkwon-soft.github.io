## About the Game
Drag to aim, release to shoot. Connect three or more of the same color to pop them — and everything hanging below falls for bonus points.

- Stage mode: levels grow from 3 to 6 colors, with up to 3 stars each
- Endless mode: new rows keep coming — how long can you hold the line?
- Every bubble carries a shape mark as well as a color, so it's easy to read for color-blind players
- Dotted aim guide that shows wall bounces
- Swap the current and next bubble anytime
- Helpers: Long Aim (an extra bounce and a landing preview) and Rainbow Bubble (matches any color)
- Records: today, this week, this month and all-time
- Pause anytime · Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
