## Info
> **Wrecking Siege** — a landscape physics game where you swing a crane wrecking ball and cut the chain mid-swing to topple castles. Free on Google Play, with ads (no banner ads).
{: .prompt-info }

## What it is
A landscape destruction game built around pendulum momentum and chain-cut timing. Smash close walls on the swing, or release the chain at the right angle to lob the ball across the courtyard.

## How to play
- Press, drag back, and release to swing the wrecking ball like a pendulum
- Tap while it swings to cut the chain and launch the ball at far structures
- Stars are awarded based on destruction rate—the share of the castle that broke or collapsed
- Wood, stone, glass, and TNT blocks react differently, while grey rock never breaks

## Good to know
- 30 handcrafted levels plus a new Daily Castle every day
- Also included inside the [FDK Siege](/posts/hub-siege/) hub app
- Plays in 9 languages with no bottom banner covering the landscape view

Free to play. Contains ads, including optional rewarded ads.
