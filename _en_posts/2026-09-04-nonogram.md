## Follow the Numbers and a Picture Appears
A nonogram (also called picross or griddlers) is a puzzle where you fill cells using
only the numbers written beside each row and column, and **a hidden picture emerges**
when you're done. The rules take a minute to learn; the small thrill of the last
cell completing the picture lasts much longer.

- 🖼️ **Hidden pictures** — fill the cells the clues describe and pixel art appears
- 🧠 **Logic only, no guessing** — every board can be solved with pure deduction
- ✅ **Fill and X modes** — mark cells you know are empty, with mistake tracking
- 💡 **Hints** — reveal one correct cell when you're stuck
- 📅 **Daily puzzle and streaks** — a new board every day keeps your run going
- 💾 **Continue anytime** — best times per difficulty and progress are auto-saved

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
