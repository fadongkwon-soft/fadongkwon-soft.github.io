## About the Game
Pyramid Solitaire deals 28 cards in a pyramid. Remove any two uncovered cards that add up to 13. The rule is simple, but which pair you take first decides whether a deal opens up or gets stuck.

## How to Play
- Remove any two uncovered cards that add up to 13. Jacks count 11, Queens 12, and Kings are 13 on their own
- When you see no pair, draw from the stock (the Draw button at the bottom) to find more. You can go through the stock three times
- Clear the whole pyramid to win
- The game tells you when no pair is left and shows your final score

## Features
- Points for every card you clear, plus a clear bonus and a time bonus
- Daily Pyramid: the same deal for everyone, with a daily streak
- Undo your moves
- Landscape button for a bigger board, and large, easy-to-read cards
- Top 10 scores for today, this week, this month and all time, plus your best streak
- Plays in 9 languages
- A few new games are free every day; watch an optional ad for more

It is free to play and contains ads.
