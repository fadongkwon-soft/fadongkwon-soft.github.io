## One Finger, One Rule
Tap the screen and the bird flaps upward; leave it alone and it drops. Every gap you
pass through is one point. **Anyone can start, but surviving for long is another
story** — the kind of arcade game that swallows a few minutes whole.

- 👆 **One-finger control** — tap to rise, release to fall
- 🐤 **How far can you fly?** — the score climbs with every gap, and so does the tension
- 💚 **One free revive** — once per run, the pipes clear and you get a brief shield to keep your record going
- 🏅 **Best score and rank badges** — earn a badge for your record and chase the next one
- 📣 **Share your score** — send a challenge to friends

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
