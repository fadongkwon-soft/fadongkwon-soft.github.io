## 1 to 25 — How Fast?
When the countdown ends, the numbers 1 to 25 appear shuffled across the board.
Find the next number, tap it, repeat; hit 25 and your time is locked in.
**That's the whole rule — but shaving off milliseconds means your eyes and hands
both have to get faster**, which is exactly why "one more round" keeps happening.

- 🔢 **1 → 25 in order** — find the next number among the scattered tiles and tap it
- ⏱️ **A millisecond race** — the next number and elapsed time sit at the top of the screen, and your best time is saved to the millisecond
- 🧠 **Focus training** — you naturally learn to use your peripheral vision
- 📣 **Share your time** — send a challenge to friends

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
