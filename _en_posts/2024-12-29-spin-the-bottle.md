## Info
> **Spin the Bottle** picks one person at the table. Choose one of nine bottles, set the group size from 2 to 12, and spin. The wheel is numbered clockwise from twelve o'clock and the winning slice turns red. Seven languages, works offline, no sign-up.
{: .prompt-info }

## You Do Not Need an Actual Bottle
There are plenty of ways to pick one person, but at a table everyone's hands are already busy.
A real bottle rolls off in the wrong direction on an uneven table, and then people argue about who it landed on.
Spinning it on screen removes that argument.

- 🍾 **Nine bottles** — soju, makgeolli, beer, wine, whiskey, vodka, gin, rum, sake
- 👥 **2 to 12 players** — the wheel splits into exactly that many slices
- 🎯 **A result you can read at a glance** — numbered clockwise from twelve o'clock, with only the winning slice in red
- 🌏 **Seven languages** — Korean, English, Chinese, Japanese, French, Italian, Spanish
- 📱 **No install needed** — on Apps in Toss it opens straight inside the Toss app

The app does not decide what is at stake. Whether it is a forfeit or the bill is up to the table; all this does is pick the person.

There is no sign-up or login, and it runs without a connection.
