## About the Game
When two of the same touch, they merge into the next size up. Keep merging before the jar fills past the line!

- Three modes in one app: Drop from the top, Shoot from below with gravity pulling up, or Numbers that double — 2, 4, 8…
- Four themes: fruit, sports balls, animals and planets
- Real physics — pieces roll, bounce and settle naturally
- Tidy Up clears the smallest pieces when you're stuck
- Records for each mode: today, this week, this month and all-time
- Pause anytime
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
