## Info
> **Number Ball Angler** — a relaxed one-button fishing game where you lower the line from your boat to collect six different numbered balls. Free on Google Play, with ads.
{: .prompt-info }

## What it is
A calm, one-button timing and collection game—no wagers, no real money, just casting your line into a drifting pond and filling out your collection book.

## How to play
- Press and hold to lower the line, release to reel it back up; the first ball it touches on the way up gets hooked
- You get 12 casts per round—collect six different numbered balls to finish
- Duplicate balls go back into the pond and cost a cast
- Golden balls award bonus points, and every catch is logged in your collection book

## Good to know
- Includes Today's Pond, shared across all players every day
- Optional rewarded ads grant 3 extra casts once per round
- Plays in 9 languages with no sign-up required

Free to play. Contains ads (a banner and optional rewarded ads).
