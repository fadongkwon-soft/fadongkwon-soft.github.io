## Info
> **Daily Korean History** opens one new question every day, drawn evenly from ten eras of Korean history. Every answer comes with a short explanation, wrong answers collect into a notebook you can re-take, and the stats screen shows which era you keep missing.
{: .prompt-info }

> The content is Korean only — questions, explanations and copy are all written in Korean, and the lottery draws it checks against are the Korean ones. It is listed in Korea.
{: .prompt-warning }

## Exactly One Question a Day
Study plans die on day three. So this app only opens one question a day.
Answer today's question and you are done. If you want more there is a "one more" button, but your streak does not need it.

Questions are spread evenly across ten eras — prehistory and Gojoseon, the Three Kingdoms and Gaya, the North–South States,
Goryeo, early and late Joseon, the port-opening period, the colonial period and modern Korea.
The difficulty sits at "something you saw in a textbook once", not at exam level.

- 📅 **Today's question** — a new one each day, with a streak count
- 💡 **Explanation included** — graded the moment you pick, followed by why
- 📖 **Wrong-answer notebook** — misses collect automatically and can be re-taken there
- ⭐ **Starred questions** — keep the ones you want to see again
- 📊 **Accuracy by era** — a table showing which period keeps catching you out

Large type, roomy answer buttons, one-handed on a train. Fully offline, no sign-up, no login.
