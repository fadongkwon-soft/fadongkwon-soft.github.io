## About the Game
FDK Arcade collects 7 arcade games: Breakout, Snake, Whack-a-Mole, Tap Bird, Sky Jump, Tower Stack and Rock Paper Scissors. Pick any of them from the home screen without installing a separate app for each.

Go for a record in one quick round. Compete on hub points in the weekly league, and complete the daily mission to keep your streak going.

## How to Play
- Tap the game you want on the home screen
- Go for a record in one quick round
- Compete on hub points in the weekly league
- Complete the daily mission to keep your streak going
- Your best record is saved for each game

## Features
- 7 arcade games in one app
- Quick rounds, built for chasing records
- Weekly league ranking
- Daily mission and streak
- Best record saved for each game
