## Reading it by suit and number

Of the four suits, Wands carries the element of fire. These are the cards that handle energy stretching from the inside outward — passion, will, action, creation, expansion. And in any suit, the Ace is the number that means a seed and a beginning. Where those two meet, the Ace of Wands draws the purest form of forward drive in the whole Minor Arcana.

In the picture, a single hand reaches out from between the clouds holding a thick wooden staff. Tender leaves are sprouting from what looks like dead wood, which says that life is already stirring even in energy this rough and unpolished. The hand coming out of the clouds shows that this inspiration was not arrived at by logical calculation; it is closer to an impulse or an intuition that turned up unannounced. Far off in the distance a castle and a river show small — the destination not yet reached. The hand only offers the staff; it does not push anybody forward. Whether to take hold of it is left to the person who drew the card.

## Upright meaning

Upright, the Ace of Wands points to the moment when what you want to do comes into sharp focus, and to the moment when you have energy enough to act on it. It is fine if the plan is still loose. What this card points to is not a finished strategy but the spark that gets you moving in the first place.

## Reversed meaning

Reversed is the state where the spark is there but will not catch. You clearly want to do it, yet for one reason or another the start keeps getting pushed back, or the early enthusiasm does not last as long as you expected. At times like that, rather than drawing up another grand plan, it works better to break the goal down into the smallest single step you could take before the day is out.

## Readings by situation

**Business** — Upright is a stretch where a new business idea surfaces and the appetite to launch it runs hot. Better to put a small prototype out into the world, something that lets you check the market's reaction quickly. Reversed is a state where ideas keep coming but execution does not follow, so the start keeps slipping; concentrating on one prototype you can actually build beats working on the big picture.

**Career** — Upright is a time when ideas for new work or a creative proposal come flashing in. A good stretch to put them forward without hesitating. Reversed points to a state where you have the appetite but the motivation to sustain it keeps scattering, so dividing the work into small, doable units helps more than launching something big all at once.

**Love** — Upright is the moment attraction toward someone flares up warmly. No reason to hold back from expressing how you honestly feel. Reversed has a sudden pull cooling just as fast, or the timing for saying something slipping past you, so building the feeling slowly beats pushing at it in a hurry.

**Study & Exams** — Upright is a time when the appetite to study rises strongly in front of a new subject or a demanding goal. Put that enthusiasm to work and start with the schedule. Reversed sees the initial passion die down within days, so starting in small, certain units — thirty minutes a day rather than one huge target — is what lasts.

**Today's fortune** — Upright is a day where new drive and inspiration keep sparking from morning to night. If something is pulling at you, better to try it right away than to put it off. Reversed is a day where intention runs ahead of action and plans easily fizzle out, so focus on one small thing in front of you rather than a grand resolution.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q. If the Ace of Wands comes up, do I have to start something new right this minute?**
Not necessarily. The card means the energy around you right now is not a bad one for starting; it is not pressure to launch into anything at speed. If an idea has already come up in your mind, read it as a sign that you have the strength to act on it now.

**Q. The Ace of Wands and the Ace of Cups both mean a beginning. How do they differ?**
The number is the same, but the element is not. True to the element of fire, the Ace of Wands points to a beginning of action and passion stretching outward, while the Ace of Cups, true to water, points to a beginning that opens up in emotion, relationship and the inner life. If you want to start something, you are nearer the Ace of Wands; if a new feeling is stirring, you are nearer the Ace of Cups.
