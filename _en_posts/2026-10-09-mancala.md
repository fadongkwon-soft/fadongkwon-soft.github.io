## About the Game
Tap a pit to pick up its seeds, then sow them one by one around the board, dropping one into your store as you pass. The game ends when one side runs out of seeds, and whoever has more seeds in their store wins.

- Classic rules: end in your store to go again, or end in an empty pit on your side to capture the seeds across from it
- Start with 4 or 6 seeds in every pit
- Three computer levels: Easy, Normal and Hard
- Two players can take turns on one device
- Choose who goes first: you, the computer or random
- Undo a move
- Win-loss-draw record for each level
- Built-in how-to-play guide
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
