## About the Game
Push the boxes onto the glowing targets. You can only push — never pull — so think one move ahead.

- 100 levels, from one-box warm-ups to five-box brain teasers
- Every level is generated and checked solvable by our own solver
- Undo and restart as often as you like
- Hints show your next push when you're stuck
- Swipe, use the arrow pad, or tap a tile to walk there
- Earn up to 3 stars per level by matching the fewest pushes
- Progress saves after every step, so you can pick up where you left off
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
