## Getting Longer Is the Difficulty
Every bite adds a segment, and that segment becomes **the obstacle for the next second.**
You get trapped by a wall you built, so there is nobody to blame.

- 👆 **Swipe to turn** — four directions, one thumb
- 🍎 **Longer with every bite** — the more you eat, the less room you have to turn
- 💗 **One free revive per run** — a single crash does not end your record
- 🏅 **Best records** — your length and score are kept
- ❓ **How to play** — controls and rules explained inside the game
- 📤 **Share** — turn a result into an image and send it to a friend

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
