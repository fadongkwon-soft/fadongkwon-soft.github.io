## Info
> **Pixel Pong** is one-on-one pong: three AI difficulties, local two-player on a single phone, and an arcade mode with power-ups you can turn off. Works offline, no sign-up. 9 languages.
{: .prompt-info }

## Two People, One Phone
Each player takes one end of the screen and faces the other. Alone, the AI takes a side.
**Paddles move by dragging sideways**, so your finger never sits on top of the ball.

- 🎚️ **Three AI difficulties** — Easy · Normal · Hard
- 👥 **Two-player** — put one phone between you and sit facing each other
- 🕹️ **Arcade mode** — power-ups fly in mid-rally
- 🎁 **Power-ups on or off** — turn them off if you want a straight match
- 🏅 **Record** — wins, losses and best scores accumulate
- 📤 **Share** — turn a result into an image and send it to a friend

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
