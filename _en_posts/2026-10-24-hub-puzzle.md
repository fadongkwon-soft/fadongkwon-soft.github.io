## About the Game
FDK Puzzle Cafe collects 11 puzzles in one app: Sudoku, Nonogram, Minesweeper, 2048, Number Slide, All Lights Off, Box Push, Make Ten, Block Fill, Merge Pop and Bubble Pop. Pick any of them from the home screen without installing a separate app for each.

Three daily puzzle missions, picked by date, sit at the top of the home screen. Finish them all to build a streak, and collect stars as you solve.

## How to Play
- Tap the puzzle you want on the home screen
- **Today's missions** at the top list that day's three puzzles. Finish all of them to keep your streak going
- Solving puzzles earns stars, and the home screen shows your streak, best streak and points
- Difficulty and records are saved separately for each game
- Leave anytime and pick up where you left off

## Features
- 11 puzzle games in one app
- 3 daily puzzle missions picked by date
- Streak and stars
- Per-game difficulty and records
- Resume a puzzle after leaving
