## About the Game
Press anywhere on the screen, push up and let go to shoot. You have 60 seconds: how many baskets can you sink? The controls take one finger, but getting the power and angle right while chaining makes is what sends your score climbing.

## How to Play
- One-finger controls: the direction you push is the shot direction, the length you push is the power. A ring and a dotted line show your aim
- Scoring: 2 points, or 3 points from behind the line, plus bonuses for clean shots, bank shots and buzzer-beaters
- Streak multiplier up to x4. At 6 in a row the ball catches fire
- The shot in the air at the buzzer still counts. Sink it for a +2 buzzer-beater
- On the start screen, tap **2D Shootout** for a regular game or **Daily Shootout** for today's game

## Features
- 7 levels: the shooting spot moves, the 3-point range opens, then the hoop moves up and down faster and faster
- Real ball physics: rim, backboard and floor bounces
- Daily Shootout: the same hoop and spots for everyone each day, with your streak and best score
- TOP 10 scores and stats
- Sound effects made on the device, with a sound on/off button
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.

## 3D Shootout Mode (1.1.0)
Version 1.1.0 adds a new mode, **3D Shootout**. Start it with the purple **3D Shootout** button on the start screen.

- Shoot from behind the player in a 3D arena. How far you push up sets the power, and tilting left or right aims
- Every time your spot changes, the camera moves around the court to stand behind you
- In levels 1 to 3 a green ring shows where the ball will drop at rim height
- From level 4 the hoop moves up and down, and from level 6 it slides sideways too
- Just like real basketball, only shots that drop through the rim from above count
- 3D scores are saved separately; switch between modes with the tabs on the records screen

## Rapid Fire (1.2.0)
From version 1.2.0 you can throw the next ball while the last one is still in the air, in both 2D and 3D.

- Balls collide: a ball that was going in can be knocked out, and a miss can be pushed in
- Every ball spins with real backspin, and the spin changes when it hits the rim, the backboard or the floor
- 3D Shootout now looks down from above the player, so you can see how far the ball travels across the court; the aiming dots cast shadows on the floor too
- In 3D, slide your finger left or right to move where you stand
- The start screen now has three buttons in one row: **Daily Shootout · 2D Shootout · 3D Shootout**
