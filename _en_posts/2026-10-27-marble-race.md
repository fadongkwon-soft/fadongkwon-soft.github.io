## About the App
A party tool for picking who pays, who goes first or who does the chore. Give everyone a marble, watch them race down the board and let the finish order decide.

## How to Play
- Type one name per line (commas work too). Add *3 after a name for 3 marbles (up to 10), or *0 to sit a round out
- Say what you are deciding, like snacks, cleaning duty or turn order, and it shows on the result card
- Pick one of 4 maps: Pin Forest, Zigzag Canyon, Windmill Hill and Moving Gates
- Choose who wins: first to finish or last to finish, with 1 to 3 winners
- Shuffle the starting spots, play at 1x or 2x speed, skip to the result or pause

## Features
- The decisive moment gets a slow-motion close-up with a highlight ring
- Result card with the order, marbles per name, date and a race code
- Watch the same race again, run a new one with the same names, or share the result as text
- Every marble uses the same physics, and only the race decides the result
- Your names are remembered on the device
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
