## The Exam Room, in Your Pocket
This is a mock question bank for people studying for the Korean nursing assistant license
(간호조무사), a national certification for assistant nursing staff. Since the 2025 revision the
written test is taken as a computer-based exam of 105 questions in 105 minutes, and the app
follows that format. Every question here is **written from scratch** and cross-reviewed, so the
explanations are thorough and there is no copyright baggage attached.

- **Practice** — choose a subject (basic nursing, public health nursing, public health, practical skills), a topic and a difficulty, then grade each answer as you go or all at once at the end
- **Full mock exam** — 105 questions on a 105-minute timer, graded against the per-subject minimums and the overall pass mark
- **Review** — missed questions collect automatically into a wrong-answer notebook, and anything you were unsure about can be starred and kept separately
- **Study record** — scores per attempt plus accuracy by subject and difficulty, with recently answered questions pushed to the back of the queue
- **Share your score** — send a mock exam result to a friend
