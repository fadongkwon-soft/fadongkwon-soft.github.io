## About the Game
Pass the phone around and let everyone pick a folded slip. Perfect for picking winners, dares or who goes first.

## How to Play
- Type the names of 2 to 20 players. Names are remembered on the device
- There are two ways to play: by number of winners (from 1 up to everyone but one), or write your own text for each slip
- Tap a slip and the paper unfolds on screen with the result. Winners get gold paper
- Cover the screen and pass it on, so nobody sees the next result early
- A result table appears at the end, and Play again shuffles the slips

## Features
- Each slip can be taken only once, so the draw is fair
- A quick dare list with light, fun penalties (no drinking)
- 2 to 20 players
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
