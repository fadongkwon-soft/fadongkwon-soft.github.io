## Info
> **Daily Money Quiz** opens one everyday-finance question a day across ten topics — savings, loans and rates, investing basics, stocks, funds and ETFs, taxes, insurance, pensions, property and consumer protection. Every answer is followed by a plain-language explanation. It recommends no products and promises no returns.
{: .prompt-info }

> The content is Korean only — questions, explanations and copy are all written in Korean, and the lottery draws it checks against are the Korean ones. It is listed in Korea.
{: .prompt-warning }

## What That Word in the News Actually Means
The difference between a deposit and an installment savings account. What happens to your loan when rates go up.
What people miss at year-end tax settlement. Things you feel you should know but will never sit down and study.
One question a day, and they add up.

Ten topics: savings, loans and interest rates, investing basics, stocks, funds and ETFs,
taxes, insurance, pensions and retirement, property, and consumer protection.

- 📅 **Today's question** — new each day, with a streak count
- 💡 **Plain-language explanation** — terms and rules unpacked on the spot
- 📖 **Wrong-answer notebook** — re-take only the ones you missed
- ⭐ **Starred questions** — keep what you want to revisit
- 📊 **Accuracy by topic** — see which subject keeps tripping you

Offline, no sign-up.

> This app is for information only. It recommends no financial product, promises no return, and is not investment advice.
{: .prompt-warning }
