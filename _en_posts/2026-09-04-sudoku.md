## Sudoku With Everything You Need and Nothing You Don't
I wanted a **daily sudoku** I could solve with my morning coffee — so I built one.
Instead of flashy effects, it carries only the features sudoku players actually use.

- 📅 **Daily puzzle** — a fresh board opens every day, and solving it keeps your streak alive
- 🎚️ **Four difficulties** — Easy · Normal · Hard · Expert. Every board has exactly one solution
- ✏️ **Notes, undo and erase** — pencil in candidates as you go, and a hint fills one correct cell when you're stuck
- 🏆 **Records** — mistake count, best time per difficulty and your last 20 games
- 🔢 **Game numbers** — every board gets a six-digit number, so you can replay it or challenge a friend to the exact same puzzle
- 💾 **Continue anytime** — your game is always auto-saved

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
