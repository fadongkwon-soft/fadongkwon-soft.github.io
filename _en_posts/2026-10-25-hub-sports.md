## About the Game
FDK Olympics brings mini golf, pool and pixel pong together in one sports game app. Pick any of them from the home screen without installing a separate app for each.

Complete the daily mission to build a streak.

## How to Play
- Tap the sport you want on the home screen
- In Mini Golf, pull back and release to putt
- In Pool, choose from 8-ball and 9-ball to four-ball and three-cushion
- In Pixel Pong, play the computer or a friend
- Complete the daily mission to build a streak

## Features
- 3 sports games in one app
- Mini Golf: pull back to putt
- Pool: 8-ball and 9-ball to four-ball and three-cushion
- Pixel Pong: play the computer or a friend
- Daily mission and streak
