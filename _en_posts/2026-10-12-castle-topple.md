## Info
> **Castle Topple: Cannon Puzzle** — Pull back, fire the cannon and topple the castle in this physics puzzle. Free to play on Google Play, with ads. Korean and English.
{: .prompt-info }

## About the Game
Pull back anywhere on the screen to aim, then let go to fire the cannon. Hit the right block and the whole castle comes tumbling down — knock down every knight guarding it to clear the level.

Blocks tilt, crack, slide and fall under real physics, so every castle collapses in its own way. The fun is in picking the one spot that brings the whole thing down in a single shot.

## How to Play
- The farther you pull, the harder the shot. Slide back to where you started to cancel
- Knights fall when they are hit hard or drop from a height. In the boss castle, the king is waiting
- Save cannonballs for stars: clear a level with shots to spare to earn up to 3 stars
- Out of cannonballs? Watch an ad for +2 and keep going right where you left off, up to twice per level

## Features
- Meadow Kingdom, 10 levels: castles of wood and stone, ending with the king's boss castle
- Three special shots: watch an ad to load an Iron Ball (breaks through even stone), a Bomb (explodes on impact, or tap while it flies) or a Triple Shot (three balls in a fan) for your next shot
- Daily Siege: one siege every day — keep your streak going
- Endless Siege: topple castle after castle, earn bonus cannonballs for clean clears and chase your best score
- Pause anytime, and turn screen shake off if you prefer
- Plays in Korean and English

More kingdoms — with ice, TNT and gold blocks — are planned for future updates.

It is free to play and contains ads.
