## About the Game
Each side hides a fleet of five ships on a 10×10 sea and fires one shot at a time into the other's waters. Find and sink every enemy ship before yours go down.

- Five ships: Carrier, Cruiser, Destroyer, Submarine and Patrol Boat. They never touch, not even diagonally, so the water around a sunk ship is always empty
- Arrange your fleet: shuffle for a new layout, or tap a ship to select it, tap again to rotate it, and tap open water to move it
- One shot per turn, even after a hit: flames mark hits, dots mark misses, and a sunk ship is revealed and crossed off the enemy list
- Three computer levels: Easy, Normal and Hard
- Choose who goes first: you, the computer or random
- Result screen with your shots, hits and ships sunk
- Win-loss record for each level
- Built-in how-to-play guide
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
