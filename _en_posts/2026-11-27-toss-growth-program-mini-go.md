On the afternoon of November 27, another welcome email arrived from Apps in Toss: [Quick Go](/posts/mini-go/) has been selected for the **Apps in Toss Growth Program**. After [Tap Bird](/posts/toss-growth-program-tap-bird/) and [Gomoku](/posts/toss-growth-program-gomoku/) in late October, this is my third app to make the list.

The promotion starts automatically on **Monday, November 30**. As before, Toss funds and runs a "browse the mini-app and earn points" promotion, then watches whether those visitors come back once it ends.

## One big change from last time

One line in the email immediately stood out:

> "You can choose the age group of users who join the promotion, or exclude users who participated recently."

Back in October with Tap Bird and Gomoku, the campaign belonged to Toss and we could not choose the audience at all—the only editable field was the landing screen. As I documented in [the D+7 results post](/posts/toss-growth-program-d7-result/), roughly three-quarters of the Benefits tab traffic was age 50 and older.

Receiving the exact same audience, Gomoku kept 1.0% (215 users) the week after the promotion ended while Tap Bird kept 0.14% (30 users)—a **sevenfold gap**. That made it unmistakable how much audience-game fit decides the outcome.

Go (Baduk) is one of the strongest natural fits for older players on Toss, and this time the console also lets us target age groups and exclude recent participants. On paper, Quick Go is starting from a much better position than the first two apps.

## Everything rebuilt in one shot before launch

With Tap Bird and Gomoku, I patched the first screen and fixed a landing-URL detection bug only after the promotion had already started, so more than 7,000 visitors saw the old home screen over the first three days. This time, I built and tested the full update **before November 30**.

**1. A Go board visible in the first 10 seconds.** The old home screen opened with a "0W 0L 0D" card and seven option tiles. Now a tappable **Go board preview** sits at the top, settings fold neatly behind `⚙️ Settings`, and the stats card only appears once you have games on record. First-time visitors arriving via `?from=growth` skip the home screen straight into an **Easy 9×9 board** (skipping the heart/ad gate on the first game, with a gentle first-move prompt). Detection checks both `initialUrl` and `location.href`, verified with a `&growthtest` switch on a real device.

**2. Daily Go Puzzle — 365 unique-solution problems.** Bringing Gomoku's Daily Puzzle loop to Go, each day unlocks a 9×9 capture puzzle solved in 2–3 moves. To avoid any copyright risk from external collections, all 365 puzzles were generated by our own Go engine and verified with an exact minimax solver to guarantee a **single unique winning first move** with `ko=-1`. Clearing daily builds a `🔥 N-day streak` and links to tomorrow's reminder (`mini-go-DAILY_20`).

**3. Hard win-streak ranking.** Connected Toss Game Center leaderboard for the longest win streak against Hard AI across 9×9, 13×13, and 19×19, using the same anti-abuse rules as Gomoku (leaving, closing, or resigning resets to 0; using Undo turns the game into practice), along with daily/weekly/monthly/all-time record tabs.

**4. Local kyu/dan rank badges.** Cumulative AI wins progress your local rank from `Novice → 18 Kyu → 15 Kyu → … → 1 Kyu → 1 Dan`, showing *"N wins to next rank"* or *"🎉 Promoted!"* on the result screen.

**5. Event tracking restored and win-only reviews kept.** Added the missing `logEvent` method to the Toss wrapper (`toss-bridge.js`) using the SDK's `{ log_name, log_type, params }` contract to track `go_growth_enter`, `go_run_start`, `go_first_move`, `go_capture`, `go_game_end`, `go_daily_clear`, and `go_notify_tap`. The fix to [only prompt for a review after a win](/posts/review-prompt-after-win/) remains in place.

## Four rounds of real-device polish

Testing the bundle directly on my phone surfaced four quick improvements to the Daily Go Puzzle:

- **Removed trivial 1-move captures (`-5` → `-6`):** Simple 1-liberty atari problems offered zero challenge for anyone who knows Go rules. Dropped all 1-move problems in favor of unique-solution 2–3 move puzzles.
- **Expanded from 120 to 365 puzzles (`-6` → `-7`):** A full year with no repeats for only +2 KB gzipped. Full-bank testing also caught and fixed a `ko` state mismatch during generation.
- **No auto-spoiler hints + real tesuji filter (`-7` → `-8`):** Removed the automatic first-move hint ring on retries and replaced it with an explicit **`💡 Show hint`** button. Filtered out simple edge-push ataris so the 365-day bank consists strictly of **non-atari vital points (nets/surrounds: 269), throw-in sacrifices (23), and multi-stone 3-move unique sequences (73)**—at most one puzzle per generated game.
- **Persistent mission bar (`-8` → `-9`):** The 2.4-second intro toast disappeared too fast while reading the board, so the puzzle goal now stays pinned directly beneath the board for the entire attempt.

## Where things stand
