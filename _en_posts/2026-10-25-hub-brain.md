## About the Game
FDK Brain Check lets you check in on your day with five games: Word Guess, Memory Cards, Sequence Memory, Reaction Challenge and Number Tap. Pick any of them from the home screen without installing a separate app for each.

See your records by area (vocabulary, memory, sequence, reaction and focus) at a glance. Finish all five daily checks to complete the day, and play every day to build a streak.

This is not a medical test. It is a game for comparing your own records.

## How to Play
- Tap the game you want on the home screen
- Finish all five daily checks to complete the day
- See your records by area (vocabulary, memory, sequence, reaction and focus) on the home screen
- Play every day to build a streak
- Time records and score records are saved separately for each game

## Features
