## About the Game
Drag pieces onto the 8×8 grid. Fill a whole row or column and it clears — clear several at once, or keep clearing turn after turn, for big combos.

- Daily Board: everyone in the world gets the same pieces today — your first run counts, so make it a good one and keep your streak going
- Classic: endless play that saves after every move, so you can pick up where you left off
- 32 piece shapes, from single squares to 3×3 blocks
- Multi-line bonuses, combo multipliers and a big Perfect Clear bonus
- Reroll the pieces in your tray when you're stuck
- Records for each mode: today, this week, this month and all-time
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
