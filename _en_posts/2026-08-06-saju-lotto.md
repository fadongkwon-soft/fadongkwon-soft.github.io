## Info
> **Saju Lotto** turns a birth date and time into a fixed set of numbers for the week — the same input always produces the same numbers, and they roll over after each draw. It covers range games like Lotto 6/45 and the Korean Pension Lottery 720+ (조 + 6 digits), with saved profiles, draw presets and 9 languages.
{: .prompt-info }

## If It Changes Every Time, It Is Just a Random Number
Plenty of apps pick lottery numbers, and most of them hand you something different on every tap.
So you keep tapping until a set looks right, which leaves you exactly where you started: choosing them yourself.

This one gives **the same numbers for the same birth details within the same period.**
They regenerate once the draw has passed. Tapping again will not change them, so there is nothing to second-guess.

- 🎂 **Deterministic draws from saju** — the traditional Korean four-pillars chart built from birth date and time
- 🗓️ **Solar or lunar, plus the birth hour** — leave the hour as unknown if you do not have it
- 🎟️ **Two lottery shapes** — range games such as Lotto 6/45, and Pension Lottery 720+ (group plus six digits)
- 🧾 **Saved profiles and presets** — keep one per family member and load it back
- 🎯 **"If you had bought last week"** — checks your set against the most recent winning draw
- 🌏 **Nine languages** — Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian

Numbers appear in the official lottery ball colors, with copy and share buttons.

> This is a generator to play with. A birth chart does not improve anyone's odds.
