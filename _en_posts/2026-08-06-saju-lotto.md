## Info
> Enter your birth date and time (solar or lunar), and Saju Lotto deterministically draws
> your personal lucky numbers for the week — the same input always gives the same numbers.
> Supports Lotto 6/45-style range games and the Korean Pension Lottery 720+ (조 + 6 digits),
> with country-specific default presets, saved profiles, and 9 languages.
{: .prompt-info }

## Features
- Deterministic draws built from your saju, the traditional Korean four-pillars birth chart made of your birth date and time — the same details always produce the same numbers
- Range-style lotteries such as Lotto 6/45, plus the Korean Pension Lottery 720+ (group + number)
- Save and reload saju profiles and draw presets, with a default preset per country
- Results shown in the official lottery ball colors, with copy and share buttons
- 9 languages (한국어, English, 日本語, 中文, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português, Italiano)
