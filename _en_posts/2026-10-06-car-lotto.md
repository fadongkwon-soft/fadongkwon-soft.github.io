## Info
> **Car Number Lotto** turns a licence plate into this week's lottery numbers. The same plate always produces the same combination within the same week, several cars can be saved side by side, and saved numbers are checked automatically against every past Korean draw.
{: .prompt-info }

> The content is Korean only — questions, explanations and copy are all written in Korean, and the lottery draws it checks against are the Korean ones. It is listed in Korea.
{: .prompt-warning }

## From a Number You Already See Every Day
There is no right way to pick lottery numbers, so people use birthdays, dreams, or the auto button.
This app uses the licence plate you look at every morning.

Type in a plate and it shows the combination that plate produces. The same plate always gives
**the same combination within the same week** — there is no reroll button. Your car gets one set this week.

- 🚗 **Several cars** — save them with names and switch between them
- 🎰 **Two lotteries** — Korean Lotto 6/45 and Pension Lottery 720+
- 🔖 **Saved numbers** — checked automatically against every past draw
- 📊 **Your luck, replayed** — the app re-runs your plate against **every past round** and counts what you would have won
- 🗓️ **Last week** — the most recent round's result sits right on the home screen

That replay screen is the most honest part of the app. Run any method across 1,200-plus rounds and it is
mostly fifth prizes and misses, with almost nothing above third. The app shows those numbers as they are.

> Numbers are for fun. Nothing is guaranteed, and the app is not affiliated with any official lottery operator.
{: .prompt-warning }
