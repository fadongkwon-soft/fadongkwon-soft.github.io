## About the Game
Draw, aim and release. Read the wind, steady your breath and put every arrow in the gold.

## How to Play
- One match is 3 ends and 10 arrows: 30 m, 50 m and 70 m, with a top score of 100
- Press anywhere and pull down to full draw (you hear the clicker)
- Move your finger to aim, release to shoot
- Watch the wind compass, wind speed and the flag on the range. The wind shifts a little every arrow and pushes farther at long distance
- Breath meter: right after full draw the sight shakes, it settles when you hold, and your arm trembles if you wait too long

## Features
- Real target scoring: 10 rings plus the X ring. An arrow touching a line counts the higher ring. Miss the face and you score M
- Today's Wind: the same wind for everyone each day, with your daily streak and best score
- Tie-breaks by X count, then number of 10s
- Arrow flight, hits and sounds are all generated on the device, with a sound on/off button
- TOP 10 scores and stats
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
