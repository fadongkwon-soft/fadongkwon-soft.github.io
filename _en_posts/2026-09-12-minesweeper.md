## I Wanted a Minesweeper That Works on a Phone
Minesweeper lives or dies on **input**, not rules. Small cells mean you tap the neighbour and lose,
and reaching for a flag detonates a mine. Those two came first.

- 🎚️ **Three difficulties** — Easy · Normal · Hard
- 🚩 **Flag mode** — turn it on and taps place flags, so a slip never ends the run
- 🔍 **Cell size** — make the grid bigger, even in the middle of a game
- 📅 **Daily challenge** — a new board every day with streaks
- ⏱️ **A timer that holds** — leave the app and come back without the clock drifting
- 💾 **Continue anytime** — your board is auto-saved

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
