{: .prompt-info }

## About the Game
Press anywhere on the screen, pull back and let go to putt. Sink the ball in as few strokes as you can across 27 hand-made holes in three themed courses.

Every hole has its own obstacles — slopes, sand traps, water, ice, spinning windmills, moving blocks and portals. Timing your shot through the windmill blades is half the fun.

## How to Play
- The farther you pull, the harder the ball rolls. Watch the power ring and the aim line before you let go
- Land in the water and it costs one penalty stroke — you replay from where you hit
- Retake: undo the shot you just played. 2 free per round, more after watching an ad
- In vs Computer and Friends modes every ball stays on the course and you take turns stroke by stroke

## Features
- 3 courses x 9 holes: Garden, Desert and Ice Cave (finish one to unlock the next)
- Solo mode with par, birdie and hole-in-one badges and a scorecard after every hole
- Vs Computer: Easy, Normal or Hard, with 1 to 3 computer players
- Friends mode: 2 to 4 players share one phone
- Quick 3-hole rounds or the full 9 holes
- Best scores for each course and a win/loss record against the computer
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
