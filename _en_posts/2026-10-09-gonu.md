## About the Game
Gonu is a family of traditional Korean games where pieces step one point at a time along the lines. Trap your opponent so they can't move — or, in Cham Gonu, line up three to capture a piece.

- Three boards: Well Gonu (2 pieces each), Pumpkin Gonu (3 each) and Cham Gonu (12 each)
- Vs Computer: Easy, Normal and Hard AI
- Pick who moves first: you, the AI or random
- 2-Player mode on the same device
- Tap a piece to see where it can move
- Undo your last move
- Win/loss/draw record for each board and difficulty
- How-to-play guide for all three boards, with tips
- Plays in 9 languages

It is free to play and contains ads.
