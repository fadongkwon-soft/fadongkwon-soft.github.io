## Short Rounds You Keep Restarting
The rule fits in one line: **slide, and matching numbers double.**
Building it reminded me why something this simple holds attention for so long.

- 🎯 **One 4×4 board** — the run ends when nothing can move or merge
- ↩️ **Undo** — take back a slide you meant to make in another direction
- 📅 **Daily challenge** — everyone starts from the same board each day
- 📊 **Statistics** — best score, highest tile reached and games played
- 💾 **Continue anytime** — your board is auto-saved
- 📤 **Share** — turn a result into an image and send it to a friend

It works fully offline with no sign-up or login, and supports 9 languages.
