## Info
> **Save Instead of Lotto** puts two numbers side by side: what weekly lottery tickets cost and won against real Korean draws, and what the same weekly amount would have become in a savings account at a rate you set. It does not tell you which is right.
{: .prompt-info }

> The content is Korean only — questions, explanations and copy are all written in Korean, and the lottery draws it checks against are the Korean ones. It is listed in Korea.
{: .prompt-warning }

## Two Numbers, Side by Side
Five thousand won a week is 260,000 a year and 1.3 million over five years. What that bought is calculable —
run it against the real draws. What the same money would have become in a savings account is calculable too.
This app puts both on one screen.

It does not argue for either side. A lottery ticket is entertainment, and spending money on entertainment is fine.
It is just better to know the price.

- ⚖️ **One screen** — total spent and won against principal-plus-interest
- 🎚️ **Period and games** — one year to every round on record, one game a week to a hundred, recalculated instantly
- 💰 **Your own rate** — simple or compound, with the 15.4% Korean interest tax on or off
- 🔖 **How your numbers did** — saved sets scored across every real round
- 📅 **Real draw results** — checked against the official published numbers

First to third prize amounts vary by round, so long-run averages are used. Fourth and fifth are fixed and counted exactly.

> The rate is an assumption you choose and guarantees nothing. No financial product is recommended; this is information, not advice.
{: .prompt-warning }
